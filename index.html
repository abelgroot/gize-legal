Gize

Gize is a personal task, time and study tracker. It runs on the owner's own private server and in the owner's own browser and phone. It is not a public service.

What Gize does

  • Tracks tasks, projects, time and study sessions.
  • Helps with focus: timers, sprints, daily priorities and reminders.
  • Optionally adds the tasks you choose to your own Google Calendar, with a phone reminder, so your plan also appears in the calendar you already use.

How Gize uses Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Gize asks for one permission: to see and edit events on your calendars (the calendar.events permission). It only creates, updates and removes the events for tasks that you tick "Add to Google Calendar" on. It does not read, copy or analyse your other events.

You can disconnect at any time from Gize's settings, or remove Gize's access in your Google Account permissions.
