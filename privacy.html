Gize privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Gize is a personal task and time tracker run by its owner on the owner's own private server. This policy explains what information Gize handles, especially when you connect your Google account.

What Gize stores

Your tasks, projects, time entries, study records and settings are stored on your own devices and on your own private servers. They are not sent to the Gize developer or to any third-party analytics, advertising or cloud service.

Google user data

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Gize requests access to your Google account email address and to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events permission.

  • What we access: your account email (to show which account is connected) and calendar events that Gize itself creates for tasks you mark "Add to Google Calendar".
  • What we do with it: create, update and delete those events, and set their reminder. Nothing else.
  • Where it is stored: the Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on your own server. Event identifiers are stored so Gize can update or delete the same event later.
  • Sharing: Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any AI model. No human reads it.

Gize's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Calendar in Gize's settings at any time. Events already added stay in your calendar until you delete them.
  • Revoke Gize's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask for your data to be deleted by emailing the contact below; because your data lives on your own server, you can also delete it yourself at any time.
